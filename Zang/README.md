## LLM-based synthetic datasets
The folder [`data_construction`](https://github.com/nicksnlp/Cantharellus/tree/main/Zang/data_construction) contains materials relevant to data synthesis. [`prompt_final.txt`](https://github.com/nicksnlp/Cantharellus/blob/main/Zang/data_construction/prompt_final.txt) contains the prompt for LLM-based data construction, [`span_construction.py`](https://github.com/nicksnlp/Cantharellus/blob/main/Zang/data_construction/span_construction.py) contains the script to convert raw outputs into the desired format, and the folder [`constructed data`](https://github.com/nicksnlp/Cantharellus/tree/main/Zang/data_construction/constructed%20data) contains synthetic datasets in 13 languages, including:

AR - Arabic

CA – Catalan

CS – Czech

DE – German

EN – English

ES – Spanish

EU – Basque

FA – Farsi

FI – Finnish

FR – French

HI – Hindi

IT – Italian

ZH – Chinese
